Private by design

The work happens on your device.

Paperhaus never sees your files. The site sends your browser the tools, and your phone or computer does the rest.

Files stay on your device

Merging, converting, signing and scanning all run in your browser. Unfinished scans are kept on that device until you clear them.

Every engine served from Paperhaus

No outside services, trackers or CDNs. The heavy lifting comes from proven open-source engines:

LibreOfficeGhostscriptPyMuPDFTesseract OCROpenCVpdf-lib

Only invited people get in

Sign in with your email and a one-time code. No passwords to remember, and you stay signed in for a month.

No app store

Pin it to your home screen or dock.

Paperhaus opens in its own window like an app, with nothing to install or update.

  1. Open Paperhaus in Safari and tap Share.
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open it from your home screen and sign in once.
